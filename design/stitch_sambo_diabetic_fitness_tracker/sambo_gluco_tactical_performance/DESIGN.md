## Brand & Style

This design system drives a mission-critical hybrid interface engineered for elite combat sambo athletes managing glycemic dynamics under acute exertion. The design language merges military-grade telemetry HUDs with the aggressive discipline of combat athletics. 

The aesthetic operates on a tactical athletic dark paradigm. Deep midnight charcoal substrates eliminate optical fatigue in low-light gymnasiums, locker rooms, and ringside benches. Micro-accents draw direct inspiration from traditional Sambo combat uniform colors (Kurtka Blue and Kurtka Red), grounded by high-precision clinical data signifiers.

The visual tone is uncompromising, rapid, and disciplined:
- **High-Velocity Contrast:** Instantaneous cognitive parsing of blood glucose velocity during rounds.
- **Instrument Precision:** Structured data grids, tactical edge chamfers, hairline separators, and concentrated metric anchors.
- **Physical Tension:** Tight layout densities, condensed athletic header typography, and restrained ambient radiance reflecting immediate metabolic reality.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate operational hierarchy across high-stress combat drills and metabolic calibration.

### Tactical Surfaces & Neutral Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F17`):** Pure deep midnight slate serving as the ground plane.
- **Card Surface (`#131B26`):** Primary interactive grouping layer; neutral, deep, non-reflective.
- **Elevated Surface (`#1C2636`):** Heads-up overlay, telemetry drawer, and modal substrate.
- **Structural Border (`#223249`):** Hairline edge boundary providing shape definition without visual weight.
- **Text & Telemetry Neutral High (`#F8FAFC`):** Primary stats, critical numeric displays, active states.
- **Text Neutral Muted (`#94A3B8`):** Units of measure, timestamps, secondary labels.
- **Text Neutral Subdued (`#475569`):** Grid marks, inactive segment labels, graph tick borders.

### Combat Signatures & Metabolic Telemetry Accents
- **Primary — Sambo Blue (`#2563EB`):** Match clock controls, session active states, standard operational CTAs.
- **Secondary — Kurtka Red (`#E63946`):** Round transitions, high-intensity exertion markers, peak cardiac thresholds.
- **Tertiary — Kinetic Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Active insulin on board (IOB), hydration metrics, rapid recovery tracking.
- **Safe Glycemic Target (`#10B981`):** In-range glucose bracket (80–140 mg/dL), operational readiness.
- **Metabolic Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Rapid glycemic delta (single arrow rise/fall), approaching operational thresholds.
- **Hypo Danger Crimson (`#EF4444`):** Acute hypoglycemia alert (<70 mg/dL), immediate combat stoppage indicator.

## Typography

Typography balances athletic aggression with clinical telemetry reading.

### Headline System
All display titles, round counts, and operational modes use Barlow Condensed. Set to bold and extra-bold weights, this condensed architecture maximizes information density without sacrificing scale. Headlines should be rendered in uppercase for session titles, timer values, and metabolic zones.

### Telemetry & Numerical Data
Numerical outputs (mg/dL, mmol/L, heart rate BPM, heart rate variability, round timers) prioritize Barlow Condensed in tabular numbers to avoid layout jitter during high-frequency live sensor streaming. Telemetry units sit strictly adjacent or stacked below values in uppercase Manrope.

### Body & Data Explanations
Manrope provides ergonomic readability for medical logs, nutrition breakdowns, and combat notes. Its geometric structure counters the aggressive verticality of the headline font, ensuring balanced UI navigation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a condensed 12-column fluid grid on tablet/desktop views and a 4-column structured grid on mobile devices.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Mobile Handheld (Combat Telemetry Mode):** Margin is pinned to `0.75rem` (`12px`) to maximize physical sensor graph visibility. Gutters run at `0.75rem`. Live dashboards stack single-column stat cards with high vertical density.
- **Tablet / Mat-side Display:** Margins scale to `1.5rem`, gutters to `1rem`. Uses a split layout: left 7 columns render live continuous glucose trend graphs and exertion overlays; right 5 columns isolate vital triage stats, cardiac zone, and active round timers.
- **Desktop (Coaching & Analytical Review):** Margins expand to `2rem`, gutters to `1.5rem`. Deep analytical tables, timeline overlays, and round-by-round glycemic variability matrices sit in standard 12-column spans.

### Layout Rhythm
Inner component layouts obey an absolute 4px-aligned stepping rhythm. Dense metric panels deploy `space-xs` (`4px`) and `space-sm` (`8px`) internal gaps to bind metric readouts to their contextual units. Containers enforce `space-md` (`16px`) padding for clear touch targets during active athletic sweat states.

## Elevation & Depth

This system discards heavy dropshadows in favor of low-contrast technical borders and focused luminescence (glow rings and ambient metric backdrops).

### Surface Layers
- **Ground (Base 0):** Background surface `#0B0F17`. Non-interactive, zero elevation.
- **Tactical Cell (Tier 1):** Background surface `#131B26` with a `1px` solid border in `#223249`. No shadow.
- **Active Interactive / Card Header (Tier 2):** Background surface `#1C2636` with a `1px` border in `#223249`. Elevated state for sliding telemetry cards and active match monitors.
- **HUD Modal / Critical Alert (Tier 3):** Background surface `#1C2636` with a `1px` high-contrast border in the trigger's state color (`#EF4444` for hypo danger, `#2563EB` for fight control).

### Radiant Telemetry Glow
Real-time indicators utilize constrained ambient radiance rather than traditional shadows:
- **Target In-Range Halo:** `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`
- **Hypo Warning Flare:** `0 0 24px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)`
- **Sambo Primary Focus:** `0 0 12px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`

## Shapes

The design system uses a strict **Soft (Level 1)** shape radius profile, evoking milled aluminum tactical hardware, biometric sensors, and protective combat equipment.

- Standard buttons, telemetry badges, input controls, and data cards utilize `rounded` (`0.25rem` / `4px`).
- Modal sheets, main sensor graph containers, and outer match monitors utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- Status indicator dots, biometric heart rate pulsars, and segmented icon toggles preserve circular geometry where required for immediate visual recognition.

## Components

### Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary Action (Sambo Blue):** Background `#2563EB`, text `#F8FAFC`, uppercase Barlow Condensed bold, `0.25rem` radius, `12px 20px` padding. Active state drops brightness by 10%.
- **Emergency Action (Kurtka / Alert Red):** Background `#E63946`, text `#F8FAFC`. Deployed for combat stoppage, quick-carbohydrate bolus logging, and timer reset.
- **Secondary / HUD Button:** Background transparent, border `1px` solid `#223249`, text `#94A3B8`. Hover/focus transitions border to `#2563EB` with text `#F8FAFC`.

### Telemetry Metric Cards
- Background `#131B26`, border `1px` solid `#223249`, internal padding `16px`.
- Large numerical readout in `telemetry-value` (`Barlow Condensed 36px/700`) aligned left or centered.
- Small uppercase metadata tags (`Manrope 11px/600`) pinned to the upper-right corner.
- Status indicator: Left border edge features a `3px` solid accent stripe representing metabolic zone status (Emerald, Amber, Crimson, or Blue).

### Combat Badge Chips
- Compact, high-contrast indicators for tracking stance, match phase, and metabolic status.
- Background `#1C2636`, border `1px` solid `#223249`, text `badge-label` (`Barlow Condensed 13px/700`, uppercase).
- Active state fills background with `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` and border `#2563EB`.

### Segmented Session Controllers
- Multi-state controllers (e.g., Warmup, Drilling, Sparring, Recovery).
- Enclosed in a `#0B0F17` track with `4px` padding and `0.25rem` radius.
- Inactive segments: text `#94A3B8`, background transparent.
- Active segment: text `#F8FAFC`, background `#1C2636`, border `1px` solid `#223249`.

### Input Fields & Rapid Entry Steppers
- Background `#0B0F17`, border `1px` solid `#223249`, text `#F8FAFC`, `0.25rem` radius.
- Focus state switches border to `#2563EB` with a `0 0 0 1px #2563EB` tactical inner stroke.
- Plus/minus steppers for rapid insulin unit and carb adjustments during mat breaks feature large hit areas (`44px x 44px` minimum) with tactile tactile states.

### Biometric Graphs & Trend Lines
- Vector trend lines sit on a `#131B26` field with hairline `#223249` grid coordinates.
- Glucose target corridors (80–140 mg/dL) highlighted with subtle fill: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.05)`.
- Trend trajectory lines use a `2px` stroke with real-time color transitions matching active blood glucose delta velocity.
